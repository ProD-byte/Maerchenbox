TITLE
Fairy Tale Box - 30 animated fairy tales on a round ESP32 display (DIY, no coding)

DESCRIPTION
30 classic fairy tales as little music-box films - drawn live and set to music by a tiny ESP32-S3 with a round 1.75" AMOLED touch display. Red Riding Hood, the Brave Little Tailor, the Town Musicians of Bremen, Rapunzel, Cinderella and many more, with speech bubbles to read along, a fairy tale book to browse and settings for parents. In English or German. New fairy tales arrive by themselves over the internet - more are on the way.

Build your own in about 5 minutes - no coding, no software to install: flash it straight from your browser.

What you need:
- Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75C
- a USB-C cable with data lines
- a computer with Chrome or Edge

Flash it here: https://daveslab.de/basteln/
Online flasher: https://prod-byte.github.io/Maerchenbox/
Source code (free & open source): https://github.com/ProD-byte/Maerchenbox
